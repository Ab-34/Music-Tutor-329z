("Rondo alla Turca"). Typeset with LilyPond from the IMSLP edition by the Mutopia Project (maintainers
Rune Zedeler and Chris Sawer). Released into the **public domain**.

- Piece page: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=108

pickup. `examples/rondo_demo.py` uses the pickup and the first 8 bars as the reference.
